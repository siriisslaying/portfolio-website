Social Media Management

I built and managed @siriisslaying from zero, turning a niche gaming page into an active community. From day one, I directed the visual identity, planned the content calendar, and kept up a consistent publishing cadence across carousels, reels, and stories.

Posts are built specifically for Instagram browsing habits, focusing on clear visual hooks and strong pacing. I experimented with various formats—from indie game recommendations to co-op roundups—refining what worked based on saves, shares, and watch time.

Generated over 1 million organic views in 60 days, with 94.9% of total reach coming from non-followers through algorithmic discovery.

Followers grew by 61.7% in 30 days entirely through organic reach, converting casual viewers into an active community without paid promotion or engagement groups.

Engagement went beyond passive likes. Indie game developers actively joined the conversation on posts, creating authentic interaction between creators and players.

Official gaming publishers, including Valorant South Asia, regularly engaged with and reshared content, establishing the page as an authentic voice in the gaming community.
