Methodology

How I work

01

Research

Every brand has a different audience and a different goal. I start by understanding both, along with the brand's existing presence, before deciding what needs to be done.

02

Planning

I develop and plan content ideas, captions, CTAs, and hooks around audience interests and the brand, then organize everything into a content calendar for consistent execution.

03

Create & Execute

I create content that aligns with the brand's identity, communication style, and marketing goals, maintaining consistency across platforms.

04

Performance Tracking

I track content performance, engagement, and audience response to understand what's working, what's not, and where there's room to improve.

05

Refine & Grow

I use those insights to improve future content, experiment with new approaches, and make more informed decisions as the brand grows.
